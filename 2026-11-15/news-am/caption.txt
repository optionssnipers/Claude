Bitcoin just hit a 4-month low, Snipers, and about $2 billion in crypto bets got wiped out. 🎯

Here's the story: Bitcoin fell to $62,112 in Asian trading on Sunday, its weakest level since July 14, reports say. Coinglass tracked about $2B in crypto liquidations over 24 hours, and US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw nearly $3B in net outflows from Oct 30 to Nov 13.

Here's what it means for YOU:
📊 Your portfolio: spot Bitcoin ETFs sit in many brokerage accounts, so crypto swings can show up there.
💵 Your wallet: coins are not bank deposits, so a drop can shrink what you hold. Nothing changes for cash in the bank.
🎯 Your edge: forced selling can speed up moves, so size positions for volatility, not just direction.

Questions you may have:
❓ Why is Bitcoin falling? Reports point to ETF outflows and forced liquidations.
❓ Is my money at risk? Crypto and crypto ETFs can swing hard, and coins are not bank deposits. Cash in the bank is not affected today.
❓ What should I watch next? Whether Bitcoin holds near $60,000, which Investing.com calls a major liquidation zone, plus weekly ETF flow data.

Sniper lesson: when too many people borrow to bet one way, a small move can force everyone out at once.
