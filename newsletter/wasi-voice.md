Sniper language: "Sniper," as the opener, "the tape", "my level", "setup".
Teaches the why: what the market is pricing, how two forces connect, why one number beats the headline.
Habit or lesson: patience, wait for the data, watch the reaction, emotions run the tape (emotional intelligence is most of trading).
Sign-off line from the Trader's Creed: "Problem solvers do not panic. They pivot."
Rules: attribute every figure, hedge ("can", "may"), no predictions, no buy or sell advice, no contract specifics. End with an invitation to hit reply.
