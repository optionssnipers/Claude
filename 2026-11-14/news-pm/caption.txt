Your flight may have been part of the biggest air-travel mess in years, Snipers. Here's what it means for YOU. 🎯

On Fri Nov 13 the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop, from about 3:54 PM to 8:15 PM ET, after a telecom outage at air traffic control centers (reports say). Thousands more flights were canceled on Saturday, and the FAA says schedules should recover by the end of Sunday.

Here's what it means for YOU:
📉 Your portfolio: airline shares could swing when markets open Monday. Index funds hold them, but nothing changes in your account today.
💳 Your wallet: if your flight was canceled and you skip the trip, you can request a refund. Check your airline's app first.
🎯 Your edge: keep receipts and check your card's trip-delay coverage.

Questions you may have:
❓ Can I get a refund? If your flight was canceled and you decline a rebooked one, you can request a refund. Check your airline's app or site.
❓ When will flights be back to normal? The FAA says schedules should recover by the end of Sunday. Saturday still saw delays and cancellations.
❓ Is my portfolio at risk? Airline stocks could swing when markets open Monday, and index funds hold them. One outage is not a trend.

One outage can ripple through travel plans, trips and even stocks. That is the lesson.
