Taiwan just hit the brakes on its stock market, Snipers, and your tech-heavy funds are connected to it. 🎯

Here's the story, per the reports: after Taiwan's TAIEX fell 6.23% on Friday, the regulator announced Saturday it is banning short selling of stocks that fell in the prior session, starting Monday, Focus Taiwan reports. The Taipei Times says the index lost almost 18% on the week. Over in the U.S., the Nasdaq closed down 5.28% on Friday.

Here's what it means for YOU:
💼 Your portfolio: Taiwan's chipmakers sit inside many US funds, so swings there can show up in your account.
🛒 Your wallet: prolonged chip-stock stress could, over time, touch prices of phones, PCs and cars.
🎯 Your edge: a ban is a market rule, not a price forecast. Know your risk before Monday.

Questions you may have:
❓ What is short selling? Betting a stock will fall. Regulators can restrict it to slow fast drops, per Focus Taiwan.
❓ Is my money at risk? Funds that hold Taiwan or chip stocks can swing. Nothing changes today, and U.S. markets are closed.
❓ What should I watch next? Monday, when Taiwan's new rules take effect and its market reopens, per Focus Taiwan.

Class is in session: a rule that limits bets on the way down changes how a market moves, but it does not decide where prices go.
