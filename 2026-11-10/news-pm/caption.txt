Inflation just came in hotter than expected, Snipers, and the Fed rate-hike talk got louder. 🎯

On Tue Nov 10, October consumer prices rose 0.4%, above the 0.3% economists expected, AP reports. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to about 5%, and traders raised bets on a December Fed hike. The S&P 500 closed down about 0.4% and the Nasdaq about 0.7%.

📉 Your portfolio: stocks slid as yields rose, and homebuilders like Lennar and D.R. Horton fell over 3%, AP says.
💳 Your wallet: higher rates could keep mortgage and card costs up over time. Nothing changes on your bills today.
🎯 Your edge: rate news can move whole indexes, so know what your funds hold.
