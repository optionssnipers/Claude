Wholesale prices just ran hot, Snipers, and your loan costs could feel it. 🎯

On Fri Nov 13, the government's October producer price report (PPI) showed prices up 0.3% for the month, a touch under the 0.4% economists expected. But the yearly rate hit 6.5%, the highest since Nov 2022, with core PPI at 5.0% a year (reports say). Market reports also show the 10-year Treasury yield rising above 5.1% on the day.

Here's what it means for YOU:
📉 Your portfolio: higher yields can weigh on stocks, and index funds hold both. Nothing changes in your account today.
💳 Your wallet: the 10-year yield often moves with mortgage and loan rates, so borrowing could stay costly.
🎯 Your edge: watch the next inflation reports. One day is not a trend.

Questions you may have:
❓ Will store prices jump? Producer prices are what businesses pay. They can reach shoppers over time, but nothing changes at checkout today.
❓ Why does the 10-year yield matter? It is a benchmark that often moves with mortgage and loan rates, so borrowing could stay costly.
❓ Is this confirmed? Yes. The BLS published October PPI on Fri Nov 13. Yield levels come from market reports.

Prices businesses pay today can become prices you pay later. That is the lesson.
