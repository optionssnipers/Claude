Futures are bouncing, Snipers, but don't pop the champagne yet. 🎯

Here's the story:
Early Monday, Nasdaq 100 futures rose about 3%, S&P 500 futures about 2% and Dow futures about 1.5% after a rough week for AI-linked shares, Reuters and Investing.com report. Both the S&P 500 and the Dow ended Friday more than 10% below their record highs.

Here's what it means for YOU:

💳 Your wallet: swings can move account balances, but nothing changes in your bills today.
🧠 Your edge: analysts cited by Reuters say a sharp bounce is hard to read as a lasting recovery.

Questions you may have:
❓ Is the selloff over? Not necessarily. Reuters cites analysts who say that with volatility still high, a sharp bounce is hard to read as a lasting recovery.
