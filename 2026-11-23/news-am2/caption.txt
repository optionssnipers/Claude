BREAKING: Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia's CEO, Snipers. 🚨

Here's the story:
Nvidia says its board voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and CEO, effective immediately. On Nov 17 the board had said it did not intend to bring him back, Decrypt reports. Nvidia also says an independent investigation continues and it is forming a new national security and export control committee.

Here's what it means for YOU:
📈 Your portfolio: Nvidia is a big holding in many index funds, so CEO news can show up in your account.
💳 Your wallet: nothing changes in your bills today.
🧠 Your edge: Nvidia says an independent investigation continues, so more headlines may follow.

Swipe to the chart: Decrypt, citing Yahoo Finance, reports Nvidia shares up more than 25% premarket Monday from Friday's close of $125.53. A premarket price is not a close and can change at the open.

Questions you may have:
❓ Is Huang really back? Yes. Nvidia says the board voted unanimously to reinstate him as CEO, effective immediately.
❓ Is the investigation over? No. Nvidia says an independent committee's probe will proceed to conclusion.
❓ What should I watch next? House committees asked for a closed-door interview Dec 1 and a Senate hearing Dec 3, Decrypt reports.
